# Soft Pastel Game Chibi

## Background Economy

- Keep the requested setting recognizable with only a few large, simple shapes. Leave broad quiet areas; do not fill empty space to make the image look complete.
- Include only explicitly requested objects or those indispensable to the action or location. Do not add incidental props, decorations, extra people, or repeated background objects.
- Keep background detail clearly lower than the subject. Do not subdivide large shapes into many small parts, patterns, surface textures, or decorative lighting effects. Preserve explicitly requested scene elements.

## Style and Request

- Create a soft, squishy super-deformed illustration with the visual mood of Trickcal: ReVive: thick outlines, pastel colors, simple forms, and flat cel shading.
- Apply the style to the requested character and setting, preserving recognizable identity and costume features.
- Follow the requested expression, action, camera angle, and framing.

## Proportions and Face

- Use the strongest deformation level in the illustration set, with a large jump in compression beyond deformed manga.
- Use approximately 1.5 to 2 heads in height: a very large round head, a compact torso, and short stubby limbs.
- Keep the silhouette soft and rounded, with expressive eyes and restrained rosy blush where appropriate.
- Give visible cheek contours a soft, slightly downward rounded fullness. In three-quarter views, let the near cheek protrude gently while the far cheek stays compact.
- Treat cheek fullness as facial shape rather than inflated cheeks; avoid elongated, tubular, or heavily swollen cheek forms.
- Keep the chin small and inset and the mouth simply drawn. Adapt mouth shape and facial acting to the requested emotion.
- Allow the head and torso to turn naturally according to the pose.

## Linework and Rendering

- Use thick clean outlines, broad simplified shapes, and minimal internal detail. Keep detail density low even for elaborate character designs.
- Construct hair from a few large unified masses without individual strands or fine hair texture.
- Construct clothing from broad flat masses with only a few essential folds and simple accessory shapes. No dense wrinkles, stitching, fabric texture, ornate eyes, or detailed anatomical modeling.
- Use flat cel coloring and cel shading only: pastel base colors and one hard-edged shadow step per material with broad, clear boundaries. Pastel color does not imply soft shading; no intermediate shadow tones.
- Limit highlights to small flat shapes.
- Avoid gradients, airbrushing, painterly blending, glossy rendering, realistic anatomical modeling, and 3D shading.

## Scene and Output

- Choose composition and crop for the requested scene rather than fixing a centered full-body view.
- Keep props and backgrounds simple and consistent with the character's low rendering density. No complex backgrounds, dense decorative detail, or complex incidental objects.
- Produce a finished game-style chibi illustration, with text or logos only when requested.
