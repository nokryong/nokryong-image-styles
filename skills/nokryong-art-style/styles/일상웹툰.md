# Everyday Serialized Webtoon

## Style and Request

- Create an image that feels like an ordinary panel from a Korean vertical-scroll webtoon episode.
- Follow the requested character, clothing, emotion, action, camera angle, framing, and setting.
- Favor the practical readability and restrained finish of ongoing serialization rather than a cover or promotional illustration.
- Low visual information density is mandatory, including for elaborate costumes or settings. No ultra-detailed or premium illustration rendering.

## Deformation Level

- Use light deformation, the weakest among the illustration styles: a subtly enlarged head, simplified facial features, and slight silhouette simplification, with torso and limb lengths remaining closer to natural proportions.
- Keep deformation visibly lighter than the shared moderate level of the other standard illustration styles. The low-detail rendering, grouped hair and clothing, and strict cel shading remain unchanged; lighter deformation does not permit realistic modeling or finer detail.

## Character and Linework

- Use simple readable silhouettes, economical facial features, minimal eyelashes, lightly described lips, and reduced anatomical and hand detail.
- Construct hair from a few large unified masses with only restrained flat highlights. No individual strands, fine hair texture, or glossy streaks.
- Construct clothing from broad flat masses with a few essential folds and simple accessory shapes. No dense wrinkles, stitching, embroidery, fabric texture, skin pores, ornate eye detail, or finely modeled anatomy.
- Use light digital linework suited to rapid serialization, omitting unnecessary detail.
- Slight repetition of forms associated with serialized production is acceptable.

## Color and Shading

- Use muted, dusty colors with restrained gray and beige tones and deep black, while preserving requested character colors.
- Use flat cel coloring and cel shading only: uniform base colors and one simple shadow step per material, with broad, hard-edged boundaries. No intermediate or secondary shadow tones.
- Do not add decorative texture or elaborate lighting effects.
- Avoid gradients, airbrushing, multistep shadows, glossy rendering, painterly effects, realistic skin detail, and 3D shading.

## Scene and Composition

- Default to a natural moment in an unfolding scene, with practical framing, readable interactions, and unforced facial acting.
- Let the requested action and camera determine perspective, motion, crop, and placement; retain the economical serialized finish in dynamic scenes.
- Keep backgrounds simple and readable using only essential location cues. No detailed scenery, complex incidental objects, or dense background textures.
- Avoid adding decorative staging or spectacle unrelated to the request.

## Output

- Produce one continuous webtoon scene by default.
- Include speech balloons, captions, sound effects, signage, or other text only when requested.
