# Low-Detail Amateur Webcomic

## Style and Request

- Create a clean, simple Korean amateur-webcomic image with the feel of early webtoons, hobby comics, and self-published internet comics.
- Follow the requested character, clothing, expression, action, camera angle, framing, and setting.
- Keep the work finished and readable but deliberately less polished than a commercial webtoon or promotional illustration.
- Low detail density is mandatory. No ultra-detailed or premium illustration finish, even when the requested character or scene is elaborate.

## Deformation Level

- Use the shared moderate deformation level: a visibly but modestly enlarged head, a compact simplified lower face, and mildly shortened, simplified torso and limbs. Keep joints and gestures coherent.
- Keep this degree of proportion exaggeration the same across the standard illustration styles, including commercial vector: stronger than the light deformation of everyday webtoon, clearly milder than deformed manga, and far milder than chibi. Each style retains its own facial design and rendering technique.

## Character and Shape

- Use a simple anime-style face, large expressive eyes, a small symbolic nose and mouth, and a smooth rounded face.
- Keep the drawing casual and slightly awkward while preserving coherent body structure within the shared moderate deformation level.
- Construct hair from a few large unified flat masses. No individual strands, fine hair texture, or elaborate highlights.
- Construct clothing from broad flat masses with a few essential folds. No dense wrinkles, stitching, fabric texture, intricate accessories, ornate eyes, or detailed anatomical modeling.

## Linework

- Use slightly uneven black lines, confident single strokes, subtle pressure variation, and economical contours.
- Avoid unfinished sketching, construction lines, doodling, cross-hatching, feathering, and excessive overlapping strokes.

## Color and Shading

- Use pure black ink and pure white paper throughout, with the existing single accent color confined to the irises.
- Construct the black-and-white regions directly as lines, white areas, and solid-black shapes. Do not desaturate a color illustration or map hair, clothing, and lighting into gray values.
- Use flat cel coloring and cel shading only. Any shadow is a sparse, hard-edged solid-black shape; do not introduce gray fills, gray screentones, or additional shades of the iris accent color.
- Keep shadows broad and simple. No gradients, textures, soft blending, grayscale painting, glossy effects, realistic rendering, or 3D shading.

## Scene and Output

- Use broad white areas and only the simplest location cues needed for the scene. No complex backgrounds, dense linework, decorative texture, or complex incidental objects.
- Adapt perspective and composition to the action while maintaining low detail density.
- Produce one webcomic scene by default. Include lettering, dialogue, signs, or other readable text only when requested.
