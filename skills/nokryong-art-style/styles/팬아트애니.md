# Low-Detail Subculture Fanart

## Background Economy

- Keep the requested setting recognizable with only a few large, simple shapes. Leave broad quiet areas; do not fill empty space to make the image look complete.
- Include only explicitly requested objects or those indispensable to the action or location. Do not add incidental props, decorations, extra people, or repeated background objects.
- Keep background detail clearly lower than the subject. Do not subdivide large shapes into many small parts, patterns, surface textures, or decorative lighting effects. Preserve explicitly requested scene elements.

## Style and Request

- Create a light, handmade 2D Japanese subculture fanart illustration using few lines, large color areas, and low rendering density.
- Follow the requested character identity, age, clothing, colors, expression, action, props, camera angle, framing, and setting.

## Deformation Level

- Use the shared moderate deformation level: a visibly but modestly enlarged head, a compact simplified lower face, and mildly shortened, simplified torso and limbs. Keep joints and gestures coherent.
- Keep this degree of proportion exaggeration the same across the standard illustration styles, including commercial vector: stronger than the light deformation of everyday webtoon, clearly milder than deformed manga, and far milder than chibi. Each style retains its own facial design and rendering technique.

## Character and Shape

- Stylize the face and body in an anime manner, with a small simple nose and mouth.
- Make expression readable through eye shape and gaze without elaborate iris detail or excessive sparkle.
- Construct clothing from broad flat masses with only a few essential folds; describe anatomy through silhouettes. No detailed wrinkles, stitching, fabric texture, or fine anatomical modeling.
- Construct hair from a few large unified masses with a small number of stray tips. Do not draw individual strands, detailed hair texture, or dense internal divisions.

## Linework

- Use a fine digital pen for clean hand-drawn lines with slight pressure-based weight variation.
- Keep contours confident and economical, avoiding mechanical uniformity and unnecessary stray strokes.

## Color and Shading

- Use flat cel coloring and cel shading only: uniform base colors and one simple hard-edged shadow step per material. No intermediate or additional shadow tones.
- Shape shadows as broad areas with hard, crisp boundaries.
- Allow only a few small, simple highlights.
- Avoid gradients, soft skin shading, rim lighting, complex illumination, excessive gloss, painterly rendering, semi-realism, and 3D shading.

## Scene and Output

- Follow the requested composition while keeping the character immediately readable.
- Keep background rendering no denser than the character. No high-density scenery, decorative textures, or complex incidental objects.
- Maintain low rendering density even when character, costume, or scene descriptions are elaborate.
- Produce a finished fanart drawing without the ornate polish of a theatrical-anime key visual or commercial beauty illustration.
