# Cute Monochrome Indie Manga — Standalone Illustration

## Document Purpose

- Create a cute monochrome manga style for standalone illustrations.
- Combine the low-density simplicity of indie manga with a slightly cuter and softer character appeal.
- Prioritize readability, charm, and clear character expression over illustration detail.

## Absolute Priority

- This style is for standalone illustrations, not for comic pages or polished commercial character illustrations.
- Keep the image simple, readable, cute, and low-density.
- Do not increase rendering density.
- Do not turn the image into a polished beauty illustration.

## Core Visual Identity

- Draw a black-and-white manga-style image with a cute, light, casual, hand-drawn feel.
- The result should feel like a simple handmade character drawing, not a detailed poster or pin-up illustration.
- Keep the drawing visually simple, expressive, and easy to read at a glance.

## Face and Character Appeal

- Make the face slightly cute and softened compared to strict indie manga.
- Use a rounded anime face, but not chibi.
- Keep the chin short and soft, but do not make the character look like a child.
- Make the eyes somewhat larger and cuter than indie manga, but still simple.
- Keep the nose and mouth very small and minimal.
- Let expression readability come first.

## Body and Proportions

- Use lightly simplified anime proportions.
- Slightly larger head than realistic proportions is allowed.
- Do not use strong glamour rendering, exaggerated anatomy detail, or body-emphasis framing.
- Keep the body concise and summarized.
- Prioritize silhouette clarity over anatomy detail.

## Linework

- Use thin black linework with a slightly rough, hand-drawn quality.
- Allow mild natural line variation.
- Do not make the lines too polished or too mechanically clean.
- Do not overload the drawing with internal detail lines.

## Hair

- Simplify the hair into a few large masses.
- Do not render hair strand by strand.
- Allow only a small number of loose outer tips.
- Keep the silhouette cute and readable.

## Shading and Color Rules

- Use monochrome only.
- Absolutely no color.
- Absolutely no grayscale painting-style rendering.
- Use only black, white, and very limited gray screentone-like values if needed.
- Prefer flat white paper, black lines, and minimal simple shadow shapes.
- Keep shading very sparse.
- If shadows are used, use only one simple hard-edged step.
- Do not use gradients.
- Do not use soft shading.
- Do not use glossy rendering.
- Do not use painterly tones.

## Single-Image Readability

- This is for standalone illustrations, so readability is more important than beauty rendering.
- Keep the requested character or characters instantly readable in one continuous scene.
- Reduce costume folds, texture, accessories, and small decorative detail.
- Use simple compositions and clear facial acting.
- Make pose and expression carry the personality or humor without relying on dialogue or panel layout.

## Background

- Keep backgrounds empty or extremely simple.
- Only add minimal location cues when necessary for the requested scene.
- Do not let the background become visually dense.

## Required Conditions

- Cute but not childlike.
- Monochrome only.
- Low detail density.
- Single-illustration readability first.
- Slightly rough handmade manga feel.
- Simpler than a normal anime illustration.
- Cuter and softer than strict indie manga.

## Prohibited Elements

- No panel divisions, comic frames, gutters, or page layouts unless explicitly requested.
- No speech bubbles, captions, lettering, or text unless explicitly requested.
- No color.
- No color accents.
- No soft grayscale rendering.
- No detailed hair strands.
- No detailed clothing folds.
- No high-density shading.
- No polished commercial illustration finish.
- No pin-up style body emphasis.
- No semi-realistic rendering.
- No excessive glamour or beauty-illustration finish.
- No dense background detail.

## Output Rules

- Output one cute monochrome manga-style standalone illustration following the user's requested content.
- The image must clearly read as a simple black-and-white handmade drawing, not a detailed polished illustration or a multi-panel comic page.
