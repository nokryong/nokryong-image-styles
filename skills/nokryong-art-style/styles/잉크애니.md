# Muted Hand-Inked Anime Illustration

## Style and Request

- Create a finished Japanese anime illustration with loose handmade ink texture, restrained color, and simple forms.
- Keep rendering density low; an elaborate subject must not produce more detailed surface rendering.
- Follow the requested character identity, skin color, clothing, expression, action, camera angle, framing, and setting.

## Deformation Level

- Use the shared moderate deformation level: a visibly but modestly enlarged head, a compact simplified lower face, and mildly shortened, simplified torso and limbs. Keep joints and gestures coherent.
- Keep this degree of proportion exaggeration the same across the standard illustration styles, including commercial vector: stronger than the light deformation of everyday webtoon, clearly milder than deformed manga, and far milder than chibi. Each style retains its own facial design and rendering technique.

## Character and Shape

- Favor thin expressive eye shapes, delicate upper eyelashes, and a small minimally defined nose and mouth. Adjust the eyes and facial acting to the requested expression.
- Preserve readable joints and gestures within the moderately deformed proportions while keeping surface detail simple.
- Construct hair from a few thick separated masses with only a small number of fine loose outer strands and simple hard-edged highlights. Do not render every strand or add internal hair texture.
- Construct clothing from broad flat masses with few essential fold marks. No dense wrinkles, stitching, fabric texture, ornate eyes, or finely modeled anatomy.
- When appropriate, use fine cheek hatching or a small flat blush shape.

## Linework

- Use thin dark hand-drawn ink lines with visible weight variation and a slightly rough, sketch-like texture.
- Strengthen selected contours around the face, hair silhouette, hands, and clothing folds.
- Keep the drawing finished and readable despite the loose pen texture; define clothing with clear filled shapes.

## Color and Shading

- Use flat cel coloring and cel shading only: muted, low-saturation base colors and one light, hard-edged shadow step per material. No intermediate or additional shadow tones.
- Place small hard-edged shadows where needed to describe forms and overlaps, keeping overall contrast low.
- Avoid gradients, airbrushing, soft blending, painterly coloring, watercolor, excessive gloss, semi-realistic rendering, and 3D shading.

## Scene and Output

- Use a clean white background by default, with composition and any necessary setting determined by the request. Reduce setting and prop detail to essential shapes; no detailed scenery or complex incidental objects.
- Do not add decorative doodles, background symbols, or handwritten marks unless requested.
- Produce a finished inked anime illustration rather than a colored sketch or fashion poster.
