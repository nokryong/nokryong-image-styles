# Lineless Rough Anime — Flat Cel Shading

## Background Economy

- Keep the requested setting recognizable with only a few large, simple shapes. Leave broad quiet areas; do not fill empty space to make the image look complete.
- Include only explicitly requested objects or those indispensable to the action or location. Do not add incidental props, decorations, extra people, or repeated background objects.
- Keep background detail clearly lower than the subject. Do not subdivide large shapes into many small parts, patterns, surface textures, or decorative lighting effects. Preserve explicitly requested scene elements.

## Style and Request

- Create a simplified Japanese indie-anime illustration built entirely from filled color shapes, with a casual handmade appearance and clear cel-shaded volume.
- Follow the requested character, clothing, expression, action, camera angle, framing, and setting.
- References define character identity and design; translate their rendering into this lineless style.
- Low detail density applies to every character, object, and background. Elaborate source designs must not increase rendering complexity.

## Lineless Construction

- Use no outlines, contour strokes, internal linework, or sketch marks.
- Define facial features, hair divisions, clothing details, and anatomical forms through boundaries between filled colors.
- Separate overlapping forms through color differences and shadow masses.
- Do not imitate outlines with narrow dark shadows or contour-following strips.

## Deformation Level

- Use the shared moderate deformation level: a visibly but modestly enlarged head, a compact simplified lower face, and mildly shortened, simplified torso and limbs. Keep joints and gestures coherent.
- Keep this degree of proportion exaggeration the same across the standard illustration styles, including commercial vector: stronger than the light deformation of everyday webtoon, clearly milder than deformed manga, and far milder than chibi. Each style retains its own facial design and rendering technique.

## Shapes and Detail

- Build forms from broad, readable, slightly organic color masses.
- Preserve expressive faces, recognizable silhouettes, and coherent anatomy while simplifying nonessential detail.
- Construct hair from a few substantial unified masses with clear silhouettes and major locks; do not add fine locks, strands, or hair texture.
- Construct clothing from broad filled masses with only a few essential fold and overlap shapes. Preserve identifying features without detailed seams, wrinkles, stitching, fabric texture, or intricate accessories.
- Keep facial features minimal; no ornate iris detail, skin texture, or detailed anatomical definition.
- Avoid strand-by-strand hair, intricate anatomical modeling, excessive seams, and fragmentation into tiny polygons or decorative patches.

## Cel Shading and Depth

- Use flat cel coloring and cel shading only: one uniform base color and one distinct, hard-edged shadow tone per material. Separate shadow regions on the same material share that tone.
- Use broad, clearly separated base-color and shadow areas, with vivid graphic color contrast that respects the requested palette.
- Shape shadows as large, simple polygons with crisp cut-out edges. Use them sparingly to clarify forms and overlaps while preserving broad uninterrupted base-color areas.
- Do not fragment shadows into small patches or add shading to describe every curve, fold, or anatomical feature.
- Avoid gradients, soft blending, intermediate shadow tones, airbrushing, bloom, glossy effects, and textured brushwork.

## Color and Scene

- Use clean matte fills with a cohesive palette and clear separation between base colors and shadows.
- Render requested settings with a small number of large flat-color shapes and essential location cues. No dense scenery, background micro-detail, or complex incidental objects.
- Apply the same lineless construction and cel shading to characters, props, and backgrounds.

## Output

- Produce a finished expressive indie-anime illustration with simple forms and convincing depth.
- Preserve the handmade character of the shapes without drifting into flat vector clip art or a fully rendered commercial anime painting.
- Add text or graphic marks only when requested.
